    How to use this page
    
    
      
  1. Pick a test section, then a fluid. Any of the fifteen sections goes with any
          of the seven fluids, or with your own properties under custom fluid.
    2. 
      
  2. Type the operating point. The form asks only for the quantities that section has;
          the eight saturation properties, the dimensionless groups and the three correlation
          anchors are computed for you and listed under Computed inputs.
    3. 
      
  3. Read the four outputs: the prediction, the two conformal bounds, and the
          OOD score, which says whether the condition resembles what the model was
          pre-trained on.
    4. 
      
  4. Inputs may leave the corpus. The prediction still comes back, with an amber line naming
          the inputs that left it — treat those as extrapolation and read the OOD score.
    5. 
      
  5. Parametric sweep varies one input and draws the response with its interval;
          Operating map varies two and draws CHF over the plane.
    6. 
      
  6. Enter an operating heat flux and Design margin reports the allowable flux
          at your safety factor, and how far one input must move to keep it.
    7. 
      
  7. Batch table takes a CSV of many operating points and gives back predictions,
          bounds and OOD scores as a CSV.
    8. 
      
  8. The unit selector at the top changes the display only. The model always works in
          kPa and kW m⁻², and a batch file is always written in those units.
